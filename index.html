02 About Us

Over five decades
of metallurgical
experience.

Since 1969, National Metal Castings has played a vital role in India's steel industry by manufacturing precision-engineered cast iron products that deliver performance, durability, and consistency.

From our manufacturing base in Mandi Gobindgarh, Punjab, we have grown into one of India's trusted manufacturers of Ingot Moulds, Forging Ingot Moulds, Rolling Mill Components, Bottom Plates, Slag Pots, and custom heavy castings.

With four integrated manufacturing units and a production capacity exceeding 1,200 tonnes per month, we manufacture ingot moulds weighing up to 30 tonnes per piece and heavy cast iron castings up to 35 tonnes per piece.

Every casting is produced using carefully selected raw materials and decades of metallurgical expertise, ensuring exceptional strength, dimensional accuracy, and extended service life.
